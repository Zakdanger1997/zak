# Picture and sound sources – i.Mak IRC video

## 3D renders
- i.Mak IRCM 93-160 L-BR geared motor and IRC 63/73/83/93/123/143 come from the official STEP models on i-Guide (https://iguide.imakreduktor.com/media/documents/3b_model/IRC/…). The IRC 103 archive on i-Guide is truncated and could not be used.
- The drum connection flange (TBF) and the DIN 5480 W70x3x30x22 spline are modelled from the dimension drawings in the i.Mak IRC catalogue 2024 (C00 2024).
- The paint colour is matched to i.Mak factory photos of blue units; catalogue IRC units are red.
- HDRI lighting is from Poly Haven (CC0): industrial_workshop_foundry, machine_shop_02, studio_small_09.
