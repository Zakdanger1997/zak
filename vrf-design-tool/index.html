Open, catalog-agnostic VRF / multi-split design workbook

Project & design conditions

Basic identification and the ambient conditions your design is rated at.

This tool does the repetitive VRF design math — combination ratio, capacity corrections, pipe sizing and refrigerant charge — using standard engineering methods. All product data (capacities, pipe tables, correction curves, charge rates) comes from the tables you enter under each tab, so results are only as accurate as the catalog figures you paste in from your manufacturer's engineering manual.

Outdoor unit & combination ratio

The combination ratio is the connected indoor capacity divided by the outdoor unit's nominal capacity. Set your manufacturer's allowable window (commonly ~50–130%).

Capacity correction

Actual delivered capacity is the nominal capacity multiplied by correction factors for equivalent piping length and indoor/outdoor height difference. Enter the curve points from your manual; the tool interpolates linearly.
