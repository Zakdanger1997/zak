# LinkedIn launch kit – The Drive Handbook

## 1. Video post (day 0)

**File:** `video/drive-handbook-4x5.mp4` (1080 × 1350, 4:5, 45 s, captions burned in, no sound needed)
**Cover image:** `linkedin/cover.jpg`
**Post from:** a person's profile (an engineer, the export manager or the owner), then reshare from the i.Mak company page with one line of your own.
**When:** Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, 10:00–12:00 Istanbul time (09:00–11:00 CET, 11:00–13:00 Gulf).

### Post text (about 950 characters; the first line works on its own before "…see more")

```
Double the gear ratio. What happens to the torque? Guess before you watch.

Most of us learned gearboxes, motors and pumps from flat diagrams. You read the formula, nod, and forget it a week later.

So we built a handbook where the diagrams stand up and move. Every page works the same way: turn the dial, make a prediction, lift the flap and check.

In the video:
1. Gear ratio: 2:1 → 4:1, same 4 kW motor. Twice the torque, half the speed.
2. Planetary stage: a 7:1 ratio inside one ring gear.
3. Motor on a VFD: why a motor at 15 Hz and full torque overheats. Its own fan slows down too.
4. Pump: 20 % slower ≈ half the power (0.8³ = 0.51).
5. Sizing: a 400 Nm mixer running 24 h a day needs a 600 Nm gear unit.

The last page is not a drawing. It is our IRKM helical-bevel geared motor, straight from the official i.Mak 3D model.

The more you play with a machine, the better you understand it.

Which one did you get wrong, and what should we explain next: worm gears, bearings or cavitation?

#Engineering #Gearbox #MechanicalEngineering
```

### Rules for the first hour

- Reply to every comment within the first 60 minutes, with a real answer (not "thanks!").
- Ask 2–3 colleagues to leave a substantive comment early (their own answer to the question), not "great post".
- Do **not** put a link in the post. If people ask for the handbook, answer in the comments.
- Do not use "comment HANDBOOK and I'll send it": since July 2026 these posts get about half the reach.

## Why this format (from the research, November 2026)

| Finding | What we did |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn's 2026 feed matches posts on topic and penalises videos whose text does not match the content | The post lists exactly the five lessons shown in the video |
| First 3 s decide the scroll; lead with an insight, not a logo | Video opens on moving gears and the question; the logo appears only at the end |
| About 80 % of feed video is watched with the sound off | Every key point is a burned-in caption |
| 4:5 (1080 × 1350) is safe on mobile and desktop | Video and carousel are 4:5 |
| Comments are worth about 2×, saves about 5× a like (AuthoredUp, 3M posts) | Ends with a real technical question; the carousel is made to be saved |
| "Comment KEYWORD" posts lost about half their reach since July 2026 (MagicPost, 684k posts) | A genuine question instead |
| Document carousels have the highest engagement rate for company pages (Socialinsider, Buffer) | A carousel follow-up from the same content |
| Personal profiles reach much further than company pages | Post from a person, reshare from the page |
| Engineers distrust "almost right" AI output | Every number is plain physics or from the i.Mak catalogue; AI is not the headline |
