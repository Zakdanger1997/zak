# i.Mak MK series key visual – "The King of Heavy Duty"

4:5 social poster (2160 × 2700) after a minimal "crowned shadow" ad concept: the MK unit stands in a red spotlight, and its shadow on the wall wears a crown.

- `poster.html` holds the layout. Run `node render.js` to write `imak-mk-king-of-heavy-duty.jpg`.
- The unit picture is i.Mak's own image from the company Higgsfield library, cut out with rembg.
- Fonts: Montserrat and Playfair Display (OFL).
- The crown is drawn in SVG in `poster.html`.
