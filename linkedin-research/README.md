# LinkedIn Company Pages — Top 100 Gearbox Distributors

Selected the **best 100 companies** from the World Distributor / Gearboxes database (400 companies, 70 countries), prioritizing verified status, manufacturers & exclusive/authorized distributors, and broad country coverage (all 70 countries represented).

LinkedIn company pages were sourced two ways and cross-checked:

1. **Web search** — each page verified against company name + country + website domain.
2. **Apollo.io** — bulk organization enrichment by website domain (90 domains, credits approved by user).

**Result: 68 of 100** companies now have a LinkedIn company page (Apollo matched 61, web search 54; combined coverage 68). The remaining 32 have no page in either source (small regional distributors or firms with no Apollo record / no website in the sheet) and were left blank rather than guessed.

Where the two sources disagreed, the **web-verified** URL was kept when it was more location-specific (e.g. *Stober France* vs Apollo's US *Stober Drives*; country-specific *Rossi* pages vs the Italian HQ). Apollo was used to fill gaps web search missed. Two Apollo domain-matches are flagged **(verify)** in the source column as possible mismatches.
