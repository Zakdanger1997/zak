# GCC Cement Cold-Call Database (100 companies)

Built from Apollo.io on 2026-09-28. **Contact-level files (emails, phones, names) are NOT committed to this public repository** – they are git-ignored and were delivered directly to the user.

## Files (delivered, not in git)
- `GCC_Cement_ColdCall_Database_100.xlsx` – 4 sheets: Master 100 · Top 50 Phone List · Email Export 100 · Rejected & Reserve
- `1_master_100_companies.csv` – 31-column master table (rank, tier, score, company, category, activity, products, country, city, website, domain, employees, revenue, industry, SIC/NAICS, company phone, contact, title, priority, department, location, email, status, best phone, phone type, LinkedIn, Apollo URLs, score breakdown, phone-list flag, notes)
- `2_email_export_100.csv` – 100 verified emails
- `3_phone_export_top50.csv` – 50 companies with usable Apollo phone numbers
- `4_rejected_and_reserve.csv` – why candidates were dropped + reserve companies

## Files in git (no personal data)
- `company_master_100_no_pii.csv` – companies, categories, scores, contact role titles only
- `DATA_QUALITY_REPORT.md` – full counts, method, audit
