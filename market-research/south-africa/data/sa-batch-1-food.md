# SA Batch 1 — Food Sector Machinery OEMs (South Africa)

**Client:** I-MAK Reduktor (industrial gearboxes / geared motors)
**Sectors:** Dairy/meat · Beverage/winery/bottling · Bakery & milling · General food processing/mixing · Packaging
**Method:** 5 parallel research agents; each company cross-checked against ≥2 sources. CONFIRMED vs INFERRED labels; blanks not fabricated.
**Unique companies after dedupe:** 42 (merged: Filmatic, Acepak, Gossamer, Facet, GVTEC, Basil BBO each appeared in 2 sub-sectors).

**Incumbent to displace:** **SEW-Eurodrive dominates the SA installed base** (local assembly in Nelspruit + Johannesburg) — the default OEM drive supplier. Also present: NORD, Bonfiglioli, Bauer, SM-Cyclo, Flender. All supplier attributions INFERRED unless noted. SA food-machinery is a thinner OEM space than Denmark; milling, packaging and mixer/agitator builders are the strongest clusters.

## SA Batch 1 highlights

- **Top tier (score 9):** Filmatic, Acepak, Macadams. Multi-machine line builders (filling, end-of-line, bakery) with high geared-motor counts and strong export.
- **Milling cluster (real & export-active):** Roff, ABC Hansen Africa, Maize Master, Facet — turnkey maize/grain mills with many conveyor/elevator/roller drives. Durable repeat-purchase base.
- **Mixer/agitator builders (product = gear-driven):** Mixtec (*openly buys 3rd-party gearboxes — ideal*), Afromix, Jones, Arcrite, STEGEL, PACCO, IPS, MaxMix. Every agitator ships with a drive head.
- **Packaging/conveying (gearbox-intensive per machine):** Powercon, Egli, NPS, Gossamer, Autopac, Calculus-TAC, PMD, Mechaneer (documents worm-gear drives).
- **Extrusion:** CFAM (heavy reduction gearboxes on twin screws) — technically the deepest single-machine gearbox fit.

## Notes vs Denmark
- SA food-machinery is a **thinner OEM space** — agents excluded many importers/agents (correctly). 42 genuine manufacturers found.
- **SEW-Eurodrive is the dominant SA incumbent** (local assembly) — I-MAK's angle is price/lead-time on standard helical, worm and helical-bevel gearmotors.
- Cross-link of note: **Filmatic** is owned by Denmark's **Trepko Group** (Batch 1 DK #13) — potential relationship lever.
- Firmographics (revenue/employees) are largely undisclosed for these private SMEs — mostly INFERRED; verify via CIPC/credit bureau before qualification.
