# South Africa Machinery OEM Market Research — for I-MAK Reduktor

**Client:** I-MAK Reduktor (industrial gearboxes / geared motors)
**Goal:** Identify South Africa-based machinery manufacturers (OEMs) that use industrial gearboxes / geared motors.
**Approach:** Same 6-batch model as Denmark. One batch per turn. Note: SA's OEM strengths differ — **mining, materials handling & bulk/mineral processing are the giants**; food is smaller. Batch skeleton adapted below.
