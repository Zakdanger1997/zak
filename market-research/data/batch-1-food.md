# Batch 1 — Food Sector Machinery OEMs (Denmark)

**Client:** I-MAK Reduktor (industrial gearboxes / geared motors)
**Sectors covered:** Dairy · Meat & poultry · Fish & seafood · Bakery & confectionery · Beverage/brewery/bottling · General food processing, mixing & packaging
**Method:** 6 parallel research agents; each company cross-checked against ≥2 independent sources (company site + CVR/proff.dk/northdata/trade press). Facts labelled CONFIRMED or INFERRED; blanks left blank, not fabricated.
**Unique companies after dedupe:** 49 (13 raw duplicates merged — see note at end)

All "Existing Gearbox Suppliers" are **INFERRED** — no company publicly documents its drive vendor. SEW-Eurodrive, NORD, Bonfiglioli, Bauer, Flender, Lenze and Sumitomo are the standard incumbents in Danish/EU food machinery and represent the competitors I-MAK would displace. Danfoss (DK) supplies VFDs/drives to most but **not** the mechanical gearboxes — an opening for I-MAK.

Score = buying-potential 1–10 (gearbox consumption × machine complexity × export activity × custom engineering).
