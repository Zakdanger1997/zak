**Sectors covered:** Feed mills · Grain handling/drying/cleaning · Livestock (pig & cattle) · Poultry & egg-farm equipment · Farm machinery & implements
**Method:** 5 parallel research agents; each company cross-checked against ≥2 independent sources (company site + CVR/proff.dk/northdata/trade press). Facts labelled CONFIRMED or INFERRED; blanks left blank.

## Batch 2 highlights (by opportunity)

- **Top tier (score 9–10):** Cimbria (10 — largest grain OEM), SKIOLD, ACO Funki, Samson Agro, Kongskilde Industries, Landmeco. High per-plant/per-machine drive counts + export.
- **Purest gearbox-consumers (product = gear-driven):** Cimbria (elevators/conveyors/dryers), Jema Agro & Lachenmeier Monsun (elevators/conveyors), Bredal & Bogballe (twin-disc spreader gearboxes), Cormall & Kverneland (planetary diet-mixer gearboxes), Samson/Agrometer (slurry pump + reel gearboxes).
- **Two incumbent worlds to displace:** electric geared motors (SEW/NORD/Bonfiglioli/Bauer) on feed/grain/conveying; PTO & angle gearboxes (Comer/Bondioli/Walterscheid) on field implements. I-MAK's angle can differ by segment.
- **Group/buying-cluster notes:** SKIOLD group owns **Landmeco (#6), Damas (#17), JYDEN (#32)** — one commercial approach. **Samson Agro** owns Pichon (France). **ACO Funki** = Egebjerg. Two separate "Kongskilde" entities: **Industries (#5, grain, independent)** vs **Agriculture (#35, CNH-owned)** — don't conflate.
- **Ownership caveats (captive/central sourcing → lower win-prob):** Andritz (corporate), Kverneland (Kubota), Kongskilde Agriculture (CNH), Sukup Europe (US), Cimbria (AGCO — though DK plants build in-house).
- **Entity-status flag:** Graintec (2023 bankruptcy) — verify before outreach.
