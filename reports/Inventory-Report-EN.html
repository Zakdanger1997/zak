Gearbox Inventory & Purchasing Study

Reconciling two China purchase invoices against the customer order book — what to buy more of, what to stop buying, and whether stock is sufficient.

SUPPLIER: HANGZHOU FIXEDSTARBUYER: I-MAK / IRASPERIOD: DEC 2025 – JUL 2026PREPARED: 11 AUG 2026

Bottom line

Stock is not insufficient — it is roughly 8× oversized. You have 12,900 gearboxes on order from China against 1,623 customer order lines. About $420,612 — 86% of the gearbox value — is surplus beyond recorded demand. Genuine shortages exist, but they total only 143 pieces / ≈$5,040. The action is not to buy more; it is to stop, rebalance, and buy nine small flange combinations.

01What the three files actually contain

The two Fixedstara files are commercial invoices from Hangzhou Fixedstar to I-MAK. File 1 is invoice FS2606043L dated 9 Jun 2026, totalling $238,991.25. File 2 carries no invoice number, is dated 17 Jun 2026 and totals $257,628.46. Every line in both files was parsed and re-summed; both reproduce the printed invoice totals exactly, so the extraction is verified.

They are not duplicates of each other — they cover different order references, with one exception. Order FCM260701 (four input-flange lines, 400 pcs, $1,520) appears identically in both files and has been counted once. Orders WX4847 and WX5008 are split across the two files, each carrying different lines of the same order — those are genuine complements, not duplicates.

The third file, SP_SIPARISLER, is the customer order book: 1,623 rows, one order line each. It contains only three columns — model, ratio, IEC flange — and the last two are mostly empty.

02Method and the one assumption that matters

The customer file has no quantity column, no dates, no prices and no customer names. Each of the 1,623 rows is treated as demand for 1 piece. This is the single assumption the whole study rests on, so here is what happens if it is wrong:

Break-even is 7.95 pieces per order line. For the current purchasing to be justified, the average customer order line would have to be almost eight units. If you can export the quantity column from your ERP, that one field converts this study from directional to exact — but even at 3 pcs/line the portfolio is still 2.65× overbought.

Second limitation

Ratio is filled in only 134 of 1,623 customer rows — and every one of them says 7.5. IEC flange is filled in only 275 of 1,623 rows. Size-level conclusions are therefore solid; ratio- and flange-level conclusions are projections from those samples and are labelled as such.

  • All B5 flanges — 706 pcs, $51,006. Not one of the 275 customer rows with flange data asks for B5; every single one is B14.

  • Ratio i=100 — 3,289 pcs, a quarter of all stock, in the slowest ratio with no demand evidence.

07Data quality flags to resolve

  • Order FCM260701 is present identically in both files (400 pcs, $1,520). Counted once here — confirm it was invoiced once.
  • Inside file 2, blocks WX5008 and WX5183 share 45 identical (model / ratio / flange / quantity) lines totalling 287 pieces. The invoice total adds both. Verify this is a genuine repeat order and not a double entry.
  • File 2's packing list sheet itemises only 60 rows (WX4847 and WX4909) but its footer states 65,795 kg gross, 94.84 CBM and 124 pallets. The itemised detail does not cover the full shipment.
  • File 1, line 43 reads QTY:195 in its description but is invoiced at 162 pieces. The invoiced figure was used.
  • File 2's invoice sheet has no invoice number, while its own packing list quotes FS2606043L — the same number as file 1.
  • The customer file carries no quantity, date, price or customer column. Adding quantity and date is the highest-value fix available.

08Action plan

  1. 01Freeze SP50 and SP63Place no new orders on these two models until stock falls below 2× cover. This alone protects $326,000 of working capital.
  2. 02Cancel or defer what is still openAny unshipped portion of the i=100 and B5 lines — up to 3,995 pieces and roughly $101,000 combined — is the cheapest saving available.
  3. 03Place one small corrective order143 pieces across nine flange combinations, plus ~12 pieces at i=7.5. Total ≈$5,040. This is the entire genuine shortage.
  4. 04Rebalance toward SP75SP75 is 13.3% of demand but 3.8% of purchases and the only model under 3× cover. Move future SP50 budget here.
  5. 05Fix the customer dataExport quantity and order date with the order book. Cover ratios become exact instead of directional, and monthly run-rate becomes computable.
  6. 06Reconcile the two flagged overlapsConfirm FCM260701 and the WX5008/WX5183 repeat with the supplier before the next payment run.
