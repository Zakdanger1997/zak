# LinkedIn company-page benchmarks (collected 2026-11-26)

Every number below was read on the linked page on 2026-11-26. "Pub." is the page's own publication or update date.
Benchmarks use different denominators, so do not compare them directly:
- **ER by impressions** = (reactions + comments + shares [+ clicks]) / impressions. This is LinkedIn's native "engagement rate" and what most 2026 studies report.
- **ER by followers** = engagements / followers. This is what can be computed for competitors from public pages (see `competitors.json`, "engagement per post per 1,000 followers" = ER by followers x 1,000, without shares).

## 3. Posting frequency and timing

| Topic | Number | Source (pub. date) |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency vs per-post performance (same accounts, vs 1 post/week) | 2-5 posts/wk: +1,182 impressions/post, +0.23 pp ER; 6-10/wk: +5,001, +0.76 pp; 11+/wk: +16,946, +1.40 pp (engagements per post "nearly triple"); no reach penalty for posting more found | Buffer, 2 M+ posts / 94 k accounts: https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin/ (pub. 2025-08-28) |
| Frequency recommendation | 2-5 posts/wk "sustainable", 6-10 to accelerate, 11+ to maximise if quality holds | Buffer, same |
| LinkedIn's own figure | Pages that post weekly grow followers ~**7x** faster than pages that post monthly (LinkedIn one-pager; vendor claim, older material) | LinkedIn follower growth one-pager: https://ppc.land/content/files/2024/07/lms-linkedin-follower-growth-best-practices-one-pager.pdf (2024) |
| Best times (Buffer) | weekdays **3-8 p.m. local**; top slots Wed 4 p.m., Fri 3 p.m., Fri 4 p.m.; best days Wed > Thu > Fri; Mon/Tue lowest; 6-11 a.m. weaker | Buffer, 4.8 M posts: https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-linkedin/ (pub. 2026-09-09) |
| Best times (Sprout Social) | Tue 11 a.m.-5 p.m., Wed 11 a.m.-4 p.m., Thu 11 a.m. and 1-5 p.m.; best days Tue-Thu; weekends worst (local time) | Sprout Social, ~2 B engagements, 307 k profiles, 2025-11-27 to 2026-02-27: https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-linkedin/ (pub. 2026-03-31) |
| Hootsuite | highest LinkedIn ER at **2 posts/week** overall (3.56%); manufacturing group best at 2 or 20 posts/week | Hootsuite (above) |

The two timing studies disagree (Buffer: late afternoon; Sprout: late morning to mid-afternoon). Overlap: Tue-Thu, roughly 11:00-17:00 in the audience's local time.

## 4. Employee advocacy

| Number | Source (pub. date) |
|---|---|
| Personal profiles: **63%** higher average ER than company pages (2026) | Metricool 2026 press release (above) |
| Employee networks average ~**10x** a company's follower count; when ~3% of employees share, company content engagement rises ~30%; employee shares get ~2x click-through | LinkedIn (Elevate materials, 2016-era): https://business.linkedin.com/marketing-solutions/blog/linkedin-elevate/2016/the-business-benefits-of-greater-employee-advocacy ; compiled by Vulse https://vulse.co/employee-advocacy-statistics (upd. 2026-08). Dated LinkedIn figures, no 2025-26 update found |
| Advocacy programme respondents report ~8x the reach of corporate channels (survey of 187 programme managers/advocates, Aug 2025-Jan 2026) | DSMN8 2026 benchmark, as compiled by Vulse (above) |
| Executive posts averaged 5,241 impressions vs 2,642 for other employee advocates in the same organisations | DSMN8 executive voice report: https://dsmn8.com/blog/executive-voice-report-key-findings/ (from search summary, not re-read) |

Multipliers vary from ~1.6x (Metricool) to 8-14x (vendor compilations) depending on metric. The most defensible 2026 number is Metricool's +63% ER for personal profiles.

## 5. 2026 algorithm changes relevant to company pages

| Change | Source (date) |
|---|---|
| 12 Mar 2026: LinkedIn rebuilt feed ranking with LLM-based "Generative Recommenders" that weigh "signals you choose to share, like your industry, skills, experience, and geography" plus engagement over time; aims for "more posts that offer genuine insight, actionable ideas, and thoughtful perspectives" (relevance over popularity) | LinkedIn Newsroom, "How LinkedIn Is Improving the Feed": https://news.linkedin.com/2026/ImprovingTheFeed (2026-03-12) |
| Demoted: recycled and click-driven posts, engagement bait ("comment to agree"), videos that don't match the post text; crackdown on comment automation, engagement pods and third-party tools | Same LinkedIn Newsroom post |
| Engineering detail: five retrieval systems replaced by one LLM dual-encoder; sequence-based ranker over 1,000+ past interactions | Secondary coverage: https://searchengineland.com/linkedin-updates-feed-algorithm-llm-ranking-retrieval-471708 ; https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/how-linkedin-replaced-five-feed-retrieval-systems-with-one-llm-model-at-1-3 (2026) |
| Platform-wide decline: views **-50%**, engagement **-25%**, follower growth **-59%** YoY (Richard van der Blom, Algorithm Insights report; secondhand, report is paid and was not read directly; Dataslayer attributes it to the 2025 edition, Perroly to the 2026 7th edition, 1.3 M posts) | https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now (2026-02-12); https://www.perroly.com/blog/linkedin-organic-2026-what-works (2026-09-23) |
| Same secondhand sources: documents ~6.6% ER, native video (30-90 s) ~5.6%, image ~3.2%, text ~2.0% | Dataslayer (above), table attributed to van der Blom 2025, LinkedIn and Sprout |
| Company-page organic posts reach ~1.6% of followers; company content is 1-2% of the feed (van der Blom 2025 edition, secondhand via search summary; not verified) | Search summary of https://leadmagic.io/gtm-skills/linkedin-algorithm (unverified) |
