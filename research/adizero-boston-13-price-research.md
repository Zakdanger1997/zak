## The shoe in brief

The **adidas Adizero Boston 13** is a lightweight, tempo/daily-trainer road running shoe. It sits between adidas' plush daily trainers and the racing-focused Adios Pro line — a "do-everything" trainer built for uptempo runs, long runs, and even racing.

- **Category:** Lightweight trainer / tempo shoe
- **Key tech:** Dual-density Lightstrike Pro + Lightstrike foam, with **glass-fiber-infused EnergyRods** (nylon rods) for propulsion
- **Use case:** Daily miles, tempo/threshold sessions, long runs, and half/full marathons
- **MSRP:** **$159.95 / $160**
- **Reviews:** Well-regarded by RunRepeat and Doctors of Running (2026) as a versatile, durable "jack-of-all-trades" trainer
